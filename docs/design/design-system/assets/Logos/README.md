# Logos

The mark is a letter P with two trailing layers and a leaf at its foot. Three inks: `logo-purple` (#693ba7, same as `brand-500`), `logo-red` (#f42000) and `logo-maroon` (#5b1004). There is no wordmark file: the name is set in Inter 600 beside the mark.

- `logo_progno.svg` — The vector source (an Inkscape sheet holding the colour and the black-and-white versions). Export from it; do not redraw the mark.
- `logo.png` — The mark, tightly cropped, transparent ground, 612×636. Footer and general use, on white, `page` or light tints.
- `logo_navbar.png` — The mark at 38×40 for the navbar, shown at 28px beside the wordmark.
- `transparent.png` — The mark centred with clear space on a transparent 1440 square.
- `batch_color_ON.png` — The mark in full colour on a white 1440 square: app icon on light grounds.
- `batch_color_OFF.png` — White P on a `brand-500` square, trailing layers and leaf kept in red and maroon: app icon and avatar on brand grounds.
- `batch_b&n_ON.png` — Black-and-white version on white: black P, layers in #666666 and #a3a3a3. Single-colour print and stamps.
- `batch_b&n_OFF.png` — The black-and-white version inverted, for dark grounds.
- `email.png` — The mark at 1400×1455, transparent ground, used in transactional emails.

Keep the mark on white, a light tint or `brand-500` (with the `OFF` version). On `surface` in dark mode the maroon layer disappears: use `batch_b&n_OFF.png` or `batch_color_OFF.png` there. Never recolour, outline or rotate it.
